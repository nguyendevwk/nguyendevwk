## Research Focus

* **Vietnamese NLP:** Semantic extraction, text processing, and enhancing Large Language Model (LLM) reliability and reasoning.
* **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR):** Vietnamese speech recognition architectures, acoustic modeling, and dataset curation.
* **Text-to-Speech (TTS):** High-fidelity Vietnamese speech synthesis and natural prosody modeling.
* **Algorithmic Optimization:** Applying GA, PSO, and HGWO for complex constraint-based scheduling problems.
* **Computer Vision:** Designing and deploying end-to-end CV pipelines for real-world applications.
